Hi, I'm Sherif Mehanni.

I run Steady Path Careers myself, from Monmouth County, New Jersey. There is no team behind me. When you work with Steady Path, you work with me. See my LinkedIn profile.

My background

I come from B2B sales, insurance and retail management.

Why I started Steady Path

Job seekers deserve openings that someone has actually checked, with clear pay, and documents built from their own facts. Too often they get stale listings and resumes that undersell them. I started Steady Path to do that part properly.

How I work

  • I find openings that fit you and check that each one is still open.
  • I tailor your resume and cover letter with help from an AI assistant, and I review every line.
  • You approve everything before it is used.
  • You submit every application yourself.
  • I usually reply within one business day.
