The ground rules, in plain words.

What I do, what I don't, and what we each agree to.

Who I am

Steady Path Careers is a job search help service run by Sherif Mehanni in Monmouth County, New Jersey. In these terms, "I" and "me" mean Sherif Mehanni and Steady Path Careers, and "you" means the person using the service. You can reach me at hello@steadypathcareers.com.

Who can use this

You must be 18 or older. The service is for people looking for work in New Jersey or the New York City area, people moving to either state, and people looking for remote jobs anywhere in the US.

What the service is

  • I look for public job postings that fit what you told me.
  • I check that each job is open on the date I check it, on the employer's own posting when there is one.
  • I tailor your resume and cover letter for the jobs you pick.
  • I help you prepare for interviews and, if you ask, review an offer with you.

What the service is not

  • I don't apply for jobs for you.
  • I don't contact employers or recruiters for you, and I never log into your accounts.
  • I don't promise any job, interview, offer or pay.

Your information must be true

Everything you tell me must be true, and yours to share. I only put facts on your documents that you told me are true. If something changes, please tell me. I may stop work on a document if I think it is not accurate.

Documents and AI use

I use an AI assistant (Claude, by Anthropic) to help draft documents, check them and research employers. I read every line before you see it. AI can make mistakes, which is one reason you review and approve everything. The tools I use are listed in the privacy policy.

You approve every document and submit every application

Nothing is final until you approve it in your portal or in writing. You decide where to apply, and you submit every application yourself. You are responsible for what you send to employers.

Job postings are public and may close or change

The jobs I send come from public postings. An employer can close, change or fill a job at any time, even right after I check it. Pay is what the employer posted, or a clearly labeled estimate. I don't control employers, their postings or their hiring decisions.

The free pilot

While Steady Path is in its pilot, the service is free for 60 days. No card is needed. Before your pilot ends, you'll get the price in writing, and you only pay if you say yes. Nothing renews or charges on its own.

Paid service

If you choose to keep going after the pilot, the fees, refunds and how to cancel are set out in a written agreement before any charge. Nothing is charged without your OK in writing.

Privacy

How I collect, use, store and delete your information is explained in the privacy policy. It is part of these terms.

Acceptable use of the portal

Your portal sign-in link is for you only. Please don't share it or forward your sign-in emails. Don't try to reach anyone else's information, interfere with the site, or use it for anything unlawful. I may pause portal access if it is misused, and I will tell you why.

Your content stays yours

Your resume, your work history and anything you write stay yours. The documents I tailor for you are yours to use in your job search. I don't use your documents as examples or share them with anyone without your written OK.

Limits of responsibility

To the extent the law allows, I am not responsible for hiring decisions, for job postings that close or change, or for losses from documents you approved and sent. Nothing here limits rights you have under New Jersey law.

New Jersey law

These terms are governed by the laws of the State of New Jersey. If we disagree about something, please email me first. I will try to fix it with you directly.

Changes to these terms

If I change these terms, I'll update this page and the date at the top. If a change is significant, I'll tell current clients by email before it takes effect.
