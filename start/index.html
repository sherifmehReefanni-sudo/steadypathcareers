Step one of four

Tell us about you.

About 10 minutes. Have your current resume handy; a rough one is fine, fixing it is part of the job.

What happens next

Within one business day you'll get a short note from Sherif with any questions about your resume. Your first verified job matches follow once we've agreed on the titles to chase.

Your trial is free. No card needed.

What we ask and why

  • Titles you want, at least two, so the search casts wide enough.
  • Your lowest acceptable pay, so we never send you below it.
  • How you get to work and whether NYC hybrid is on the table, so commute is real, not guessed.
  • Numbers from your current job (people, accounts, dollars), because they make a resume believable.
