Privacy

Your information, handled with care.

What I collect, why, who can see it, and how to have it deleted. In plain English.

Last updated October 17, 2026

The short version

  • I collect only what I need to run your job search.
  • Your information is seen by you and me, Sherif Mehanni. No one else on my end.
  • I never sell or rent your information, and I never send it to employers. You apply to every job yourself.
  • You can ask me to correct or delete your information at any time.

Who I am

Steady Path Careers is a job search service run by Sherif Mehanni in Monmouth County, New Jersey. In this policy, "I" and "we" both mean Steady Path Careers. You can reach me at hello@steadypathcareers.com.

What I collect

  • What you tell me on the intake form: your name, phone, email, city, the work you want and don't want, pay you'd accept, your work history, education, licenses, software you've used, and anything else you choose to share.
  • Your resume, if you upload one.
  • Your activity in your portal: your Yes, No and Question answers on jobs, document approvals and change requests, application updates, and any feedback you send.
  • Emails and messages you send me.
  • Intro call bookings: if you book a call, the name, email, phone and answer you enter on the booking form.

Why I use it

Only to run your job search: finding and checking jobs that fit you, writing and tailoring your resume and cover letters, preparing you for interviews, keeping your portal up to date, and contacting you about your search. I also read feedback to improve the service.

Who can see it

You and me. I don't have employees or partners who access client files. I never sell, rent or trade your information, and I don't share it with employers or recruiters. When you apply for a job, you send your own documents.

To run the service, your information is stored with or passes through a few companies that provide the tools I use:

  • Google (Sheets, Drive, Gmail, Calendar and Apps Script) stores your intake answers, resume and portal data, sends our emails, and runs the intro call booking page.
  • Anthropic (Claude, an AI assistant) helps me draft and check documents and research employers. I review everything before you see it.
  • Email delivery services such as Brevo may deliver emails sent from steadypathcareers.com addresses.
  • GitHub hosts this website. Your intake answers and resume are not stored on the website itself.
  • Cloudflare Web Analytics counts visits to the public pages of this site. It doesn't use cookies or track you across other sites, and it isn't used in your portal.

I'd only disclose your information otherwise if the law required it.

On your device

This site doesn't use advertising or tracking cookies. Your browser does keep two small items on your device: an unfinished intake form, so you don't lose your answers if you leave, and your portal sign-in, which keeps you signed in for 30 days. Signing out or clearing your browser data removes them.

How long I keep it

I keep your information while you're using the service. If you stop, I delete your resume, intake answers and portal data within 12 months of our last contact, unless you ask me to keep them so you can pick up where you left off. You can ask me to delete everything sooner at any time.

Your choices

  • See or correct your information: most of it is in your portal. For anything else, just email me.
  • Delete your information: email hello@steadypathcareers.com from the address you signed up with. I'll delete it within 30 days and confirm by email.
  • Stop emails: reply and tell me. I only email you about your own job search.

Keeping it safe

Your files are kept in password-protected accounts that only I can access. Portal sign-in uses one-time links sent to your email, not passwords. No system is perfectly secure, but I take care to limit who and what can reach your information.

Who this service is for

Steady Path Careers is for adults 18 and older. I don't knowingly collect information from anyone younger.

Changes to this policy

If I change how I handle your information, I'll update this page and the date at the top. If it's a significant change, I'll tell current clients by email first.

Questions

Email hello@steadypathcareers.com. I'm happy to answer anything.
