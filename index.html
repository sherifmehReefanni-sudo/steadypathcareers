Questions people ask

Can I talk to someone before I sign up?

Yes. Book a free 15-minute intro call and Sherif will phone you at the time you pick. No sales pitch, and you can start the intake form whenever you're ready.

What does it cost?

Nothing during our pilot. Before your trial ends you'll see the price in writing, and you choose whether to continue. Nothing renews or charges automatically, and we never ask for a card to start.

Do you apply to jobs for me?

No. You submit every application yourself, with the tailored resume and cover letter we prepare. We never log into your accounts or contact employers as you.

Can you guarantee me a job?

No one honestly can. What we promise is the process: real openings, real pay, documents that only say what's true, and a person keeping track of every step.

Will you help me negotiate my offer?

Yes. Send the offer and we'll go through pay, benefits and start date together. If asking for more makes sense, you'll get a short counter email or call script in your own words. There's no guarantee an employer will move, and the decision is always yours. See an example.

Do you redo my LinkedIn profile?

On request. Sherif rewrites your headline, About section and job entries to match your resume, and you paste them in yourself. It isn't part of the standard service and may carry a separate fee; you'll know the price before any work starts. See an example.

Who sees my information?

You and Sherif. Your resume is stored privately in our files, your portal is behind a sign-in link sent to your email, and we never share or sell your information.

How many jobs will I get?

Usually 5 to 10 a week, after we've agreed on the titles to chase. Fewer if the market is thin for your field, because we only send jobs that pass a live check.

What if a job doesn't post its pay?

We tell you. If we still include the job, we give a labeled estimate and say where it came from. New Jersey and New York both require pay ranges in most postings now, so most employers do post them.

What areas do you cover?

New Jersey and the New York City area for in-person and hybrid work, plus remote jobs anywhere in the US. Moving here from another state? Tell us on the form and we'll search where you're headed.

How do I sign in to my portal?

Use the Client sign in button at the top of this page and enter your email. We send a one-time link, no password, and it keeps you signed in on that device for 30 days.

Ready when you are.

Ten minutes to start. Then you'll hear from Sherif with a short note about your resume.
