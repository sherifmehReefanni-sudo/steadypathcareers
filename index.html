How it works

Four steps. Your portal always shows where things stand.

  1. 1Tell me about youA 10-minute intake and your current resume.
  2. 2I read your resume firstI fix what's holding it back, and we agree on the job titles to go after.
  3. 3You pick from verified jobs5 to 10 a week, each with pay and why it fits. Answer Yes, No or Question in your portal or right from the email.
  4. 4I prepare, you applyA tailored resume, cover letter and interview prep for each Yes. You send the application; I track it, remind you to follow up, help with offers, and send a weekly note.

Is this for you?

An honest answer, so neither of us wastes time.

A good fit if you

  • Live in New Jersey or the New York area, are moving here, or want remote work
  • Have work history to build on, even with gaps
  • Want fewer, better jobs instead of hundreds of links
  • Will answer Yes, No or Question within a few days

Not a fit if you

  • Want someone to apply in your name or log into your accounts
  • Need a guaranteed job or a guaranteed salary
  • Want anything on your resume that isn't true
  • Want a recruiter or an executive search firm

How I check every job

Each job passes these checks before it reaches you.

  • Live posting checkedI open the employer's own posting to make sure it's still open.
  • Pay range checkedFrom the employer's posting, or a clearly labeled estimate.
  • Location fitsThe area and work setting you picked (on site, hybrid or remote).
  • Duplicates removedThe same job on several sites reaches you once.
  • You approve everythingNothing goes out without your OK. You send every application yourself.

Questions people ask

Can I talk to someone before I sign up?

Yes. Book a free 15-minute intro call and Sherif will phone you at the time you pick, or call or text him at (732) 759-0008. No sales pitch, and you can start the intake form whenever you're ready.

What does it cost?

Free for 60 days. Before your pilot ends, you'll get the price in writing, and you only pay if you say yes. Nothing renews or charges on its own, and I never ask for a card to start.

What happens after 60 days?

You choose: keep going at the price you saw in writing, pause, or stop. If you stop and ask me to delete your files, I delete them within 30 days and confirm by email. How long I keep files.

Do you apply to jobs for me?

No. You submit every application yourself, with the tailored resume and cover letter I prepare. I never log into your accounts or contact employers as you.

Can you guarantee me a job?

No one honestly can. What I promise is the process: real openings, real pay, documents that only say what's true, and me keeping track of every step.

Will you help me negotiate my offer?

Yes. Send the offer and we'll go through pay, benefits and start date together. If asking for more makes sense, you'll get a short counter email or call script in your own words. There's no guarantee an employer will move, and the decision is always yours. See an example.

Do you redo my LinkedIn profile?

On request. I rewrite your headline, About section and job entries to match your resume, and you paste them in yourself. It isn't part of the standard service and may carry a separate fee; you'll know the price before any work starts. See an example.

Who sees my information?

I do. Your files are kept in my private Google account. I use an AI assistant (Claude, by Anthropic) to help draft and check documents, and I review every line before you see it. Your documents are shared with you by private link, so anyone who has the link can open them. Keep portal emails to yourself. I never sell your information or share it with employers. The full list of tools is in the privacy policy.

Do you use AI?

Yes, as a tool. AI helps me draft and research. I check every job myself and read every document before it reaches you, and you approve it too.

How many jobs will I get?

Usually 5 to 10 a week, after we've agreed on the titles to chase. Fewer if the market is thin for your field, because I only send jobs that pass a live check.

What if a job doesn't post its pay?

I tell you. If I still include the job, I give a labeled estimate and say where it came from. New Jersey and New York both require pay ranges in most postings now, so most employers do post them.

What areas do you cover?

New Jersey and the New York City area for in-person and hybrid work, plus remote jobs anywhere in the US. Moving here from another state? Tell me on the form and I'll search where you're headed.

How do I sign in to my portal?

Use the Client sign in button at the top of this page and enter your email. The portal emails you a one-time link, no password, and it keeps you signed in on that device for 90 days.
