Your account

Delete your Steady Path account

How to ask Steady Path Careers to delete your account and your information, and what happens next.

See the steps

Three ways to ask

  1. In the Steady Path app: open More, choose Delete my account, type DELETE and send.
  2. In your portal on this website: sign in, open More, choose Delete my account, and confirm.
  3. By email: write to hello@steadypathcareers.com from the address you signed up with, and say you want your account deleted.

We only act on a request that comes from your signed-in account or from the email address on file, so a request from anyone else is not acted on.

What happens next

Nothing is deleted right away: your account stays as it is until we act on your request. We delete your account and your information within 30 days of your request and confirm by email. That includes your resume and intake answers, your jobs, documents, applications and notes.

Once it is deleted, your job search stops and you can no longer sign in to the portal or the app. What the app keeps on your phone (your practice notes, stories, follow-up marks and saved programs) is removed when you sign out.

What we keep

If you bought a paid plan or add-on, we keep the record of what you agreed to buy, when, and which terms you were shown. Stripe, which handles payments, keeps its own payment records under its own rules.

If you have a paid plan

Asking us to delete your account does not cancel a paid plan or stop charges. Cancel it first: in your portal, open More, then Plan & billing, then Manage billing.

Questions? Write to hello@steadypathcareers.com or call or text (732) 759-0008. Our privacy policy has more detail.
