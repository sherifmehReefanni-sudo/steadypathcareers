What the app does

It is the same client portal you use in a browser, saved to your home screen so it opens full screen, like an app.

Job matches

See each new match with its pay, where it is and why it fits. Answer Yes, No or Question with one tap.

Your documents

Open your tailored resume, cover letter and interview prep. Approve them or ask for changes.

Questions

Ask about any job. The answer shows up on that job's card in the app.

Application tracker

Every application you send, and where it stands, from applied to offer.

The app needs a connection to load your latest matches and documents. It is part of the service, so there is nothing extra to pay.

Add it to your phone

It takes about a minute. No app store and no download to search for.

You're already using the app. Nothing else to install.

iPhone

Use Safari.

  1. Open steadypathcareers.com/portal in Safari.
  2. Tap the Share button (the square with an arrow).
  3. Scroll down and tap Add to Home Screen.
  4. Tap Add. Steady Path now has its own icon on your home screen.

Android

Use Chrome.

  1. Open steadypathcareers.com/portal in Chrome.
  2. Tap the menu (three dots, top right).
  3. Tap Install app, or Add to Home screen on some phones.
  4. Tap Install. Steady Path now has its own icon with your other apps.

On a computer?

Point your phone's camera at this code to open the client portal on your phone, then follow the steps for your phone above.

Signing in from the app

There is no password. The first time you open the app:

  1. Enter your email and tap Email me a sign-in link.
  2. The email has a link and a 6-digit code. Type the code into the app.
  3. That's it. The app stays signed in on that phone for 90 days.

Why the code? On an iPhone, the home screen app keeps its own sign-in, separate from Safari, so tapping the link in the email would sign you in to Safari instead. The code signs you in right inside the app. Each link and code works once.
