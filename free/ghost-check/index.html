What it does and doesn't do

It does

  • Ask the employer's hiring system if the posting is live right now.
  • Show the date the job was first posted, when that system shares it.
  • For Greenhouse and Lever, look for a second listing with the same title in the same place.
  • Answer with one of three plain labels: open on the employer's site, closed or removed, or can't check this link.

It doesn't

  • Guess. If the system gives no clear answer, it says so.
  • Decide if a job is a scam. An open posting can still be one, and a closed one may have been real.
  • Read LinkedIn, Indeed or job board copies.
  • Keep your link, ask for your email, or need an account.

How to spot a ghost job yourself

A ghost job is a posting for a role nobody is really hiring for right now: an old listing left up, or a job that was already filled. These are the checks we use:

  1. Find the same job on the company's own careers page. If it's not there, be careful.
  2. Check the posting date. A job up for months, or reposted again and again, is a warning sign.
  3. Search the job title plus the company name, and compare the dates you find.
  4. When in doubt, ask the recruiter if the role is still being filled before you spend an evening on a tailored application.
